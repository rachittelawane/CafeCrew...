The vibe

Written by whoever named the Gumnaam pizza

Every menu has a joke hiding in it — because Cafe Crew isn't trying to be fancy, it's trying to be your regular spot. Seawoods keeps it cozy for solo laptop sessions and small hangouts. CBD Belapur turns up the polish for birthday parties and bigger crews, with a free parking lot so nobody's circling the block.

Both outlets: pocket-friendly (₹200–₹400 a head), family-friendly, and open every single day.
